How a pair is priced

one café, one florist

The café's volume is a benchmark (GroCycle: 5 to 10 lb bags a day). The price is what a garden shop pays for finished compost at Dakota Prairie Composting in Shakopee, $30 a cubic yard bulk, about 3 cents a pound; bagged is 24 cents. Proximity is 1.0 within 100 m, 0.8 within 250 m, 0.6 to 400 m. Urgency 4 means the material spoils in three days: the café has to move it every day whether or not anyone asks.

The metro equation, for contrast

what the same model says when the actor is a broker clearing the whole metro

score = value term × obscurity × urgency × access ÷ competition  ·  value term: $10k → 1, $1M → 3, $100M → 5, 1.0 when unpriced
valuebest of resale, collection or substitute price × volume across every business on side A. Substitute was added 2026-09-30; before it a stream nobody resells was $0.
obscuritystill typed by hand, 1 to 5. The one term nothing derives yet.
urgencynearest of four clocks: a rule date, dated events on side A, the material spoiling, a disposal fee. The last two were added today.
accessstrongest asset we hold for the material, else the business listing at 4. A typed 3 used to hand regulated streams a second bonus.
competitioncounted incumbents, +1 for a firm over 50 years old, +1 for a national player, neutral 3 when nobody is enumerated.
